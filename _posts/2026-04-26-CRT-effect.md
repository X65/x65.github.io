Emu with CRT shader effect just left the oven and is available for download and online.

![CRT effect](/media/2026-04-26_CRT-effect.jpeg)

Although X65 has a DVI digital output,
it can be connected to a CRT monitor with a simple DVI-to-VGA adapter.  
The CRT shader effect is designed to simulate the look of a CRT monitor using the emulator.

The effect can be enabled and configured using the emulator GUI,
and using the command line.
