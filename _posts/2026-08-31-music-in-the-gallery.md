Four more music demos are available in the [web Emu](/emu/).

[![Alloyrun](/emu/roms/alloyrun.png)](/emu/?rom=roms/alloyrun.xex#Emu){:target="_blank"}

- [Alloyrun](/emu/?rom=roms/alloyrun.xex#Emu) by VOID, from Reality AdLib Tracker (`.rad`)
- [FM-Tronikk](/emu/?rom=roms/fm-troni.xex#Emu) by Malfunction/Altair, from AdLib Tracker II (`.a2m`)
- [Pinball Illusions](/emu/?rom=roms/pinball_illusions.xex#Emu) by Olof Gustafsson, from an Amiga ProTracker module (`.mod`)
- [E1M1: At Doom's Gate](/emu/?rom=roms/e1m1.xex#Emu) by Bobby Prince, from a General MIDI file (`.mid`)

Each tune was imported into SGU Tracker and exported as an SGM module bundled
with the 6502 player in an autorun XEX file. Emu runs that player as an X65
program, driving the emulated SGU-1.

These demos show the import and export path working with AdLib FM instruments,
Amiga samples and General MIDI music.
