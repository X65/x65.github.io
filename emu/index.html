--- title: X65 Computer Emulator image: /emu/screenshot.png description: Emu is a browser-based emulator for the X65 microcomputer, showcasing its graphics modes, sound capabilities, and tech demos. Built using a modular chip-emulation framework, Emu replicates the X65's hardware, including the WDC 65C816 CPU, CGIA graphics and SGU-1 synth chip. It features demos like raster bars, sprites, and music playback. Explore Emu at https://x65.zone/emu/. ---
